Maxfiylik siyosati

EpchilPOS — restoran xodimlari (ofitsiant, oshpaz, barmen, menejer va admin) uchun ish ilovasi. Ilova restoranning o‘z serveriga ulanadi va faqat ish uchun zarur ma’lumotlar bilan ishlaydi.

Qanday ma’lumotlar ishlatiladi

  • Kirish ma’lumotlari: login va parol. Ular faqat restoran serveriga kirish uchun yuboriladi. Parol telefonda saqlanmaydi; kirgandan keyin olingan kirish kaliti (token) telefonning himoyalangan xotirasida (Android Keystore / iOS Keychain) saqlanadi.
  • Xodim ma’lumotlari: ism-familiya va lavozim (rol). Ularni restoran administratori kiritadi.
  • Ish ma’lumotlari: buyurtmalar, stollar, menyu, to‘lovlar va hisobotlar. Ular restoranga tegishli bo‘lib, restoran serverida saqlanadi.
  • Sozlama: server manzili va oxirgi kiritilgan login telefonda saqlanadi (keyingi safar qayta yozmaslik uchun).

Nimalar yig‘ilmaydi

Ilova joylashuv, kontaktlar, kamera, mikrofon, fayllar yoki qurilma identifikatorlariga kirmaydi. Reklama, analitika va kuzatuv xizmatlari ishlatilmaydi. Ma’lumotlar uchinchi shaxslarga sotilmaydi va berilmaydi.

Uzatish va himoya

Ilova va server o‘rtasidagi aloqa HTTPS orqali shifrlanadi. Ma’lumotlarga faqat shu restoranning ruxsat berilgan xodimlari o‘z vazifasiga ko‘ra kira oladi.

Saqlash muddati va o‘chirish

Ish ma’lumotlari restoran hisobi va qonunchilik talab qilgan muddat davomida restoran serverida saqlanadi. Xodim hisobini restoran administratori o‘chirishi yoki bloklashi mumkin. Ilovadan chiqilganda (“Chiqish”) yoki ilova o‘chirilganda telefondagi kirish kaliti o‘chadi. Hisobingiz yoki ma’lumotlaringizni o‘chirish uchun restoran administratoriga yoki quyidagi manzilga yozing.

Bolalar

Privacy Policy

EpchilPOS is a work app for restaurant staff (waiters, cooks, bartenders, managers and admins). The app connects to the restaurant’s own server and only handles data needed for the job.

Data the app uses

  • Sign-in data: username and password, sent only to the restaurant’s server to sign in. The password is not stored on the device; the access token received after sign-in is kept in the device’s secure storage (Android Keystore / iOS Keychain).
  • Staff data: full name and role, entered by the restaurant’s administrator.
  • Work data: orders, tables, menu, payments and reports. This data belongs to the restaurant and is stored on the restaurant’s server.
  • Settings: the server address and the last username are stored on the device for convenience.

What is not collected

The app does not access location, contacts, camera, microphone, files or device identifiers. No advertising, analytics or tracking services are used. Data is not sold or shared with third parties.

Transfer and security

All communication between the app and the server is encrypted with HTTPS. Only authorized staff of the same restaurant can access the data, according to their role.

Retention and deletion

Work data is kept on the restaurant’s server for as long as the restaurant’s accounting and the law require. A staff account can be deleted or blocked by the restaurant’s administrator. Signing out or uninstalling the app removes the access token from the device. To request deletion of your account or data, contact the restaurant’s administrator or write to the address below.

Children

The app is intended for restaurant staff and is not directed at children under 13.
